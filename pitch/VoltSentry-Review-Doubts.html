Doubt 1 — "In grid oscillation, the values don't change"

You were right, ma'am. Earlier the attack only sent start/stop messages without actually drawing power, so nothing moved on the screen. Now every charger switches between full power and zero at the same moment, once a second — like a real attack meant to stress the transformer. The transformer jumps between 82 % and 0 %, the line current between 1385 A and 0 A, and each charger card flips between Charging 120 kW and Finishing 0 kW. Our rule R3 detects the pattern in about 1.5 seconds.
Press 2 in the attack tool. Point at the Site Transformer panel and the charger cards — they change every second.

Doubt 2 — "What firewall are you using?"

VoltSentry itself is the firewall — an application-level firewall we built, which understands the OCPP charging protocol. A normal firewall only checks IP addresses and ports, so it cannot tell when a charger lies — for example reporting 5 kW while using 120 kW. VoltSentry reads every message and blocks the fake ones before they reach the server. A real site uses both: its normal network firewall in front, VoltSentry behind it.
Open the Defences tab. The diagram at the top shows Chargers → Network firewall → VoltSentry → Server, with live counts of messages inspected, forwarded and blocked.

Doubt 3 — "Apart from quarantine, what other safeguards do you use?"

Several. We refuse fake duplicate charger connections, refuse chargers blocked by another site, refuse oversized or badly formed messages, and block cloned RFID cards. Messages between sites are signed and cannot be replayed, and card numbers are never shared — only scrambled hashes. New sites can start in monitor mode, and every attack is saved in a forensic log.
On the Defences tab, scroll down. Each safeguard is a card with its live count.

If she asks what is missing — say it before she does: charger passwords and certificates (OCPP security profiles) and a real login for the dashboard are not built yet. They are marked "planned" on the same Defences screen. TLS works but is switched off on the laptop demo.
